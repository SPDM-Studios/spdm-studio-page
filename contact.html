Quick answers.

04 questions
How long does a typical project take?

Six to fourteen weeks for an initial release, depending on scope. We bias toward shipping a small, real version early — then iterating in 2‑week cycles after launch.

Do you take on equity or partnership work?

Occasionally. We're picky about it — usually only when we'd build the product anyway. If that's you, mention it in the brief.

What does the engagement look like day‑to‑day?

Weekly Friday demos with a real build, a shared Linear board, and async updates the rest of the time. You'll have direct contact with the people writing the code.

Can you maintain the product after launch?

Yes — we offer a light retainer for tracked metrics, monthly polish, and feedback loops. Or we'll hand off cleanly to your team.
