The idea.

3 min read

Why we built it.

Most scoreboard apps are stuffed with menus, ads, and rules engines for sports you'll never play. We wanted something that worked the same whether you're playing 21 in a driveway or running a six‑round tennis match — large numbers, two clear taps, no friction.

The product.

My Scoreboard is a single‑purpose Android app: pick a game template (or a blank one), pick teams, start. The dark UI keeps the score the focal point, the buttons are sized for fingers in motion, and undo is one swipe away. The app runs entirely on‑device — no account, no telemetry, no internet needed.

The result.

Live on Google Play, free to use, with a small freemium layer for power users who want custom game templates. A real product we use ourselves at every studio social.
